Schedule background tasks, recurring cron jobs, or delayed commands.

Supports the following actions:
- "create": Schedule a new recurring cron job or one-shot delay command
  - Requires `name`, `command`, and either `expression` (standard 5-field cron syntax: `min hour dom month dow`, e.g. `*/5 * * * *`) or `delaySeconds` (for one-shot execution).
- "list": View all scheduled jobs, their status, last run, and next run times.
- "remove": Delete a scheduled job by `id`.
- "run_now": Trigger immediate execution of a scheduled job by `id`.
- "toggle": Enable or disable a job by `id` (using `enabled: boolean`).

Use this tool when:
- Setting up recurring health checks, tests, or monitors
- Scheduling periodic backups, reports, or sync tasks
- Running automated background jobs
